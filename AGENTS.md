# The Last Abbasid: agent briefing

Read this first in every session. Details live in `README.md` and `PROGRESS.md` (the state of the work and the next actions). Keep this file short and current.

## What this project is
- A 2D pixel-art action platformer in Godot 4.7.2 (typed GDScript), set during the Mongol sack of Baghdad, February 1258. The hero, Yusuf ibn Harun, is a guardsman of the Caliph. Historically grounded, fictional characters and events; no magic, no supernatural powers, no fantasy monsters, no caricatures.
- **Visual direction:** dark, detailed, semi-realistic pixel art at 640×360 with integer scaling; night over a burning city; firelight warm, everything else cool and dim; characters outlined and rim-lit so they read against the scenery. Original designs only; take qualities from Blasphemous, Dead Cells and Prince of Persia, never their art. The user's concept art pack (`docs/concept_art/`) sets the direction: black banners with a gold crescent, cranes and hanging cages, lattice balconies, cobalt tilework, square crenellated gate towers, dark lamellar with bronze rivets, round bronze shields.
- **Priorities:** combat feel and readability first, then art consistency, then content. A polished short slice beats a long unfinished one.

## Rules
- Typed GDScript everywhere. `project.godot` turns untyped declarations and unsafe property/method access, casts and call arguments into errors, so such a script fails to load. Read `Variant` values into typed variables before use (`var x: int = dict["k"]`), never pass them straight into typed calls.
- Controls built in code are added to their parent first, then laid out (anchors and offsets, `set_anchors_and_offsets_preset`); never set `size` or `position` on a control without a parent: it takes the direction of the language of the moment, and in Arabic it is mirrored a whole parent off to the left (the boss bar's trail, the cinematic's picture). Test right-to-left layouts by building the node while the locale is `ar`, not only by switching to it afterwards.
- Feature folders (`features/<feature>/`), shared presentation in `shared/`, the session in `app/`. No global event bus, no universal manager, no autoloads unless truly needed.
- Controllers drive bodies from outside: `WarriorInput` drives the `Warrior`; `EnemyBrain` subclasses drive `MongolSoldier`. Bodies carry rules (health, poise, guarding, staggers); brains decide.
- Shared tuning lives in read-only Resources (`WarriorProfile`, `EnemyProfile`, `AttackDefinition`). Mutable state belongs to the actor.
- Gameplay never depends on presentation. VFX and sound are wired in `app/main.gd` from signals (`struck`, `swung`, `telegraphed`, hero and soldier signals). `VfxDirector`, `SoundDirector`, `MusicDirector` know no gameplay types.
- Attack timing is animation-driven: an attack's frames (`active_from`..`active_to`, `recovery_from`, `telegraph_frame`, `lunge_*`) index its animation strip; the hitbox each active frame is the blade's swept polygon from `FrameHitboxes`, generated with the sprites.
- Generated files are generated: `assets/**` (art, audio, fonts), `features/levels/*/[level].tscn` and `app/main.tscn`. Change the generator or the data (`tools/levels/<level>.mjs`) and rebuild.
- Soldiers: swordsman, spearman (his low sweep glints amber: jump or roll), archer, keshig veteran (a swordsman whose quick cut is followed by a backhand after a held beat), mace-bearer (`EnemyProfile.unflinching`: light blows do not stop him; his overhead blow breaks a guard), Georgian shield-bearer (`shield_wall`: his tall shield turns every frontal blow, the cleave too, and a blow it turns hardly wears him (`MongolSoldier.WALL_POISE`); only an `overwhelms` blow (the bash, the charged cleave, the plunge), a parry's riposte or a blow from behind gets past, and he turns slowly), siege engineer (lobs `FirePot`s that leave `BurningGround`, unblockable fire; it burns a soldier thrown into it, `Combatant.is_reeling`), Kipchak skirmisher (a dash cut aimed to end at sword's length, `Combatant.aim_lunge`, a chained knife thrust; leaps back untouchable from a heavy blow wound up near him, `MongolSoldier.evade`, `EnemyBrain.can_leap`), Georgian axeman (the hook `pulls` the hero in and breaks a guard, the butt drives him off, the chop breaks a guard and the low sweep may follow it, the sweep is low), and the Captain (in his second phase a low sweep, `captain_sweep.tres`, under a guard held up before him; he re-plans a blow he cannot throw within 0.8 s and forgets his chain when thrown off his stroke). Soldiers fight with more than one move: a blow may be followed at once by its `follow_up` (`follow_chance`, chained: the swordsman's slash by the cut, the mace's sweep by another, the skirmisher's dash by the knife); the swordsman feints (`SwordsmanBrain`); the spearman lunges from beyond his thrust; the archer kicks; a duellist (`EnemyProfile.parries_after`, the veteran) parries the next blow of a string turned on his shield and ripostes (`riposte`). Reactions: poise builds blow on blow (`Combatant.poise_recovery_delay`); two flinches within 1.2 s steel a man for 1 s (`MongolSoldier.FLINCH_LIMIT`, `steeled`); a stagger is neither ended nor stretched by a blow; a man down is kept down longer once, and the ground stroke rouses him (`rouses`); guards come up a beat after each swing is seen (`EnemyProfile.reaction_time`, `Combatant.attack_serial`); a man open to a finisher is never thrown out of reach (`FINISHABLE_PUSH`); a level's `aggression` shortens pauses and readies guards. Arrows loosed from a roof are aimed at the hero (`Arrow.MAX_ANGLE`).
- Encounters are designed, not scattered: each fight in a level asks its own question (see PROGRESS.md). Level data gives soldiers an `activity` (an animation they loop until they notice the hero: `loot`, `burn`, `menace`, `chat`, `watch`, `execute`, `stab`), `hidden` (out of sight until their group wakes), `guard` (block chance), `group`, `patrol`, and for an executioner `victim` (a captive's id) and `delay`. A blow on a soldier who has not noticed the hero kills him (the Captain's armour excepted; a thrown knife wounds him twice over and turns him); a sprung ambusher chases the hero however far he goes; one who hears the hero behind him is startled a moment before he turns; one who notices shouts and comrades within 210 px join (not a headsman, who finishes his work). A headsman strikes `delay` seconds after the hero comes within 300 px unless killed or turned first; saved and lost captives are remembered (`saved_<id>`, `lost_<id>`).
- Gore is presentation: the body only decides what a killing blow cut off (`MongolSoldier.severed`, from the attack's `severs`/`sever_chance`, head for surprise kills and ripostes) and falls in that animation; `shared/vfx/gore_director.gd` (wired in `app/main.gd`) throws the pieces (`GorePiece`), pumps the wound (`BloodFountain`, following the wound frame by frame from the soldier's `GoreSet`), pools and splats the blood. The gore setting (full/reduced) is `GameSettings.gore` → `MongolSoldier.dismemberment` and `GoreDirector.full`. What is and is not shown: `docs/art_style_guide.md` §10.
- Fairness rules (tested): every enemy attack telegraphs at least 0.22 s before it lands (a low sweep, which asks for a jump or a roll, and the Captain's opening cut, a third of a second), by what answers it (`AttackDefinition.tell()`: white: block or parry; amber, `low`: a sweep no standing guard stops, jump or roll; violet, `guard_break`: parry or roll; red: unblockable, roll; all but white with a sign over the head and a flush held until the blow lands, each its own sound), except a chain's follow-up (`chained`); a guarding soldier turns to a hero behind him only after `EnemyBrain.GUARD_TURN`; the hero is untouchable through a finisher; at most two soldiers near the hero swing at once (`EnemyBrain.MAX_ATTACKERS`; a shot drawn in the same fight, within `SHOT_REACH`, counts; one drawn far off does not) and the others wait a step off; no blade is swung at a hero on a ledge (`MELEE_HEIGHT`); no soldier starts an attack on a hero who is invulnerable or was hit within `EnemyBrain.BREATHER`; the hero's post-hit invulnerability (0.9 s) outlasts his stagger, and a roll breaks a stagger after 0.18 s; soldiers never walk off ledges.

## Architecture in one breath
Input or AI → `Warrior` / `MongolSoldier` state machines → `Combatant.begin_attack` → animation frames open the `Hitbox` with the blade sweep → `Hurtbox` → `Combatant.receive_hit` (`judge_hit`: HIT, BLOCKED, PARRIED, DODGED, GUARD_BROKEN) → `on_struck` / `on_hit_landed` → signals → `app/main.gd` → VFX, sound, camera shake, hit-stop, HUD. The session (`AbbasidGame`) loads a `Level` into `World`, inserts the hero after the level's `Enemies` node, binds the camera and HUD, and handles lamps (`Checkpoint`), pages (`Manuscript`), people (`Npc` + `DialogueLibrary`; always spoken to: until their `requires` flag is set they say their `<dialogue>_waiting` words and nothing is remembered; `Npc.gives_flag` hands something over), captives (`Captive`), captives under a headsman's sabre (`Level.captive_killed`, `captive_saved`), triggers (`StoryTrigger`: hints, lines, and the events `refugees` (one is shot as he runs), `ambush` (wakes `group`), `alarm` (turns `group` to the hero), `boss`), groups (`Level.group_cleared` sets `<group>_cleared`) and the exit (`LevelExit`; `Level.next_level` and `exit_card` lead on through a `StoryCard`, the last level to the ending). A card with a cinematic (`CinematicDefinition.find(id)`, `assets/cinematics/<id>/`, generated by `tools/cinematics/build_cinematics.mjs` from its shot list `tools/cinematics/<id>.mjs` and the painting data in `paintings.mjs`) plays it in `CinematicPlayer` (`features/cinematics/`): each shot a painting through a moving camera (`cinematic.gdshader`: area-averaged, depth parallax held within `depthShift` screen pixels, fire, water, cloth, smoke, the map's ink, graded and mapped to the shot's palette lookup), its caption, lines (timed by length) and heading in the letterbox; it asks for sounds through the card (`cue`, `ambience`); seen ones skip at a press (`user://seen.cfg`). The story is data: each level's `objectives` (`flag|KEY|target`, first match wins; the target, `npc:<id>`, `exit`, `col:<n>:<NAME>` or `group:<g>`, is what the HUD marks) and a gate's `lockedLines` (what Yusuf says at it, by the story) come from `tools/levels/<level>.mjs`; `main.gd` holds no level-specific story. A boss fight is a `BossArena` (barriers, camera bounds, the boss) started by a `boss` trigger; `CaptainBrain` runs the Captain; the blow that would kill him beats him to one knee (`MongolSoldier.beaten`) and the session plays the finishing stroke (`_on_boss_beaten`, `Warrior.cinematic_strike`, `MongolSoldier.finish`). Archers left alone in a group flee, so a group fight always ends where the hero can see it. Breath (the hero's stamina): costs in each `AttackDefinition.stamina_cost` and `profile.roll_cost`; every blow that lands gives `breath_gain` back (`Warrior.recover_breath`); at zero he is winded (`exhausted_delay`) and an action he cannot pay for is refused (`breath_refused`); Steady Breath (the shield raised in the glint as a blow ends, `steady_window`), a Close Call (a blow met in a roll's first `close_call_window`: slow time, a counter) and a parry give breath. Weight: `AttackDefinition.knocks_down` throws a soldier down (`MongolSoldier` `State.DOWN`: falls, lies `down_time`, rises untouchable; not a steadfast man) or the hero (`Warrior.State.DOWN`); over a man down the heavy button is the ground stroke (`ground_stab`), on a wounded one the ground finisher (`FinisherDefinition.ground`); reactions `hurt_b`, `reel` (knockback >= `REEL_KNOCKBACK`), `parried`. A blow that `seeks` stretches its lunge to land `strike_at` px from the man before it (`Combatant.aim_lunge`). The hero's moves: the light string (`profile.combo`: forehand, rising backhand, thrust, and the kick that throws a man back) with an ender on the heavy button in each cut's live frames (`Warrior._ender_index`: pommel strike, whirling cut, executioner's cleave), the delayed cut (light a beat after the rising cut; `AttackDefinition.feint` makes a guarding soldier lower his shield), the heavy cleave (breaks guards) and its charge (heavy held: `State.CHARGE`, `profile.charged_cleaves` II overwhelms, III unblockable with `flinch_radius`), the heavy string (heavy again: `profile.heavy_string`, the rising cleave, then the windmill, `rehit`), down and light the low cut (`low` also passes a soldier's raised round shield, not a wall), down and heavy the reaping sweep (taught; it throws down men off their guard, not one behind a raised shield, which is the low cut's job), light on the run the running slash, light behind the shield the guarded thrust (`AttackDefinition.guarded`: he still blocks), light after a parry or a close call the riposte (`profile.riposte_attack`), the running thrust (heavy on the run, `stops_on_hit`), the Arts (`ArtDefinition` in `profile.arts`, paid with `Warrior.resolve`, played in `State.ART` on the `art` / `art_2` buttons; two carried, `art_slots`; each spent with a moment in `main.gd`: a freeze, the world drained to grey about him (`post_process.gdshader` `focus`), its name across the screen (`ArtBanner`); the Storm's three steered turns that draw men in (`steer_speed`, `pulls`) and its rising `finale`; the Line's dash and the wounds it opens (`opens_wounds` on `effect_frame`); Greek fire that sets soldiers ablaze (`BurningGround.ignites`, `MongolSoldier.ignite`: they run burning and spread it; no brain steps into fire, `covers`); the Second Wind's cry (`cry_*`, `Combatant.frighten`) and fury (`fury_*`: faster blows, blood back per blow, kills hold it); the Judgment's chain of up to three (`judgment_chain`, `Warrior._judge_next`, a blink between them)), block and parry (moving behind the shield he steps with the guard held, `block_walk` forward and `block_back` away, chosen by `WarriorAnimator.guard`), the shield bash (heavy with the shield up), the roll; in the air the slash (`profile.air_slashes` a jump; the second a backhand, `air_slash_2`), down and light the down-stab (`bounces`: he springs off what it strikes) and the plunge (`Warrior.State.PLUNGE`: the blade turned down, a drop at `plunge_speed`, then `plunge_landing`); down and jump drop him through one-way planks; the light button late in a roll is the rolling cut (it chains into the thrust); `throw` flicks a knife (`profile.max_knives`, refilled at lamps). A light blow turned by a raised shield glances off (`glances`, `Warrior._glance`, as it gives way, unless a guard-breaker was called for). Every move has one job, and is taught just before the soldiers who demand it (`docs/combat_focus_plan.md`): `lesson_*` triggers teach the low cut in the Market, the running slash in the Streets of Ash, the kick (before the engineers' fire) and the charged cleave (past the axeman) in the Scholars' Quarter; Hamid's Market counsel is the parry and riposte, shown on a card (`Npc.counsel` → `AbbasidGame._counsel`). The moves whose jobs others do are the master's techniques, closed on a first journey: the pommel strike, whirling cut, delayed cut, executioner's cleave and running thrust on the tree (`requires_flag` `master`, `LOCK_MASTER`), the rising cleave, windmill, guarded thrust and down-stab by lessons that wait on the flag (`StoryTrigger.requires_flag`); finishing the chapter records it apart from the save (`SaveGame.record_master`, `user://records.cfg`) and every later journey sets the `master` flag (`_master_journey`). A man of a dormant group busy at a task in plain sight (the library's) sees, hears and is taken unawares as any busy soldier (`EnemyBrain._at_work_in_sight`); hidden ambushers and the taskless wait blind until their trigger springs them. A move not learned leaves its buttons to the plain moves. Every press in a blow's live frames is kept for when it gives way (`Warrior._light_next`), a beat after a step of the string the light button carries it on (`string_grace`), the run's blows need a man ahead (`RUN_REACH`), the riposte's force belongs to the riposte; an action is paid in full or refused (the roll needs a breath), Steady Breath follows only a blow that met a man, a parry window keeps the next shut a while, a blocked blade chips 15%. What he can do is what he has learned (`Warrior.techniques`, `knows()`): the session sets it on entering a level from `Level.known_techniques` (what the levels before taught) and the `knows_<technique>` flags; a page of a treatise (`Manuscript.teaches`) or a gift (`Npc.teaches`) teaches one (`AbbasidGame._learn`): the sweep in the Fallen Market, the bash in the Streets of Ash, the knives from Salim, the plunge in the Scholars' Quarter, the rolling cut at the Last Gate. Finishers: a staggered soldier at half health or less glows red and the heavy button plays one of the `Warrior.finishers` (`FinisherDefinition`, generated in `assets/characters/warrior/finishers/`): the hero's `finish_*` and the soldier's `finished_*` frame-locked, the soldier set `distance` px ahead, the hero untouchable; the full one (bars, slow time in `main.gd`) when no other soldier near is in the fight (`Combatant.in_fight`, `MongolSoldier.engaged` set by his brain), otherwise quick (`Warrior.QUICK_FINISHER`); each gives back `profile.finisher_stamina`. Growth: `Progression` (`features/progression/`) is the rules over the save: Honour (`SaveGame.honour`, earned once per deed through `AbbasidGame._earn` with a flag), the technique tree (`ProgressionCatalog` `catalog.tres`: `TechniqueDefinition` nodes that `grants` a technique and/or carry `Modifiers`, gated by the node above, `requires_known` and `requires_flag` `reached_<level>`), keepsakes (`KeepsakeDefinition`; given by `Npc.gives_keepsake`, `Captive.keepsake`, or picked up as a `Relic`; worn two at once) and the two Arts carried. The session applies it (`_apply_growth`: `Warrior.set_techniques` (story + bought), `set_modifiers`, `art_slots`); the lamp menu (`LampMenu`, after every rest, `State.LAMP`) buys, unlearns and equips; the pause menu's `TechniquesScreen` shows every move performed (`MovePreview` plays the move's `MoveDemos` demo: its animations at their own timing, carried by their lunges, with the game's blade trails (`SwordTrail.strike`), its buttons lighting up in order); the lamp menu (`TechniqueDefinition.demo`) and a teaching treatise page (`ManuscriptReader.read(id, teaches)`) show it the same way. The player is told things in one way each (`docs/ux_overhaul_plan.md`): lessons (`AbbasidGame._hint`) queue on the HUD's `LessonCard` at the top centre (none lost, timed by length, first sentence in a fight); news stacks in the `NoticeStack`; lines said in passing (`_say`) are the `SubtitleLine`; a new objective is a `MomentBanner`; every one of their clocks stops while the game waits; a technique learned from the story, the first lamp and the first warning of each colour stop the game on a `LessonScreen` card (`_cards`, shown at the first quiet moment); every lesson met is kept for the pause menu's Guide (`SaveGame.lessons`, `Lessons`). Over the world the HUD's `WorldMarkers` draws a speech sign over people with something new to say (`Npc.has_news`), the objective's sign or an arrow at the screen's edge, a lamp's sign, names, and the button for what is at hand; buttons are drawn as keys (`KeyCaps`, `KeyText`, `InputGlyphs.caps`) in the text face `abbasid_text`. In play the HUD's `MoveCoach` names a learned move over the hero as its moment comes (`Warrior.open_techniques()`), with its button, until it has been used three times (`Warrior.technique_used` → `SaveGame.practice`; `GameSettings.move_prompts`: while learning, always, off). Levels make their soldiers tougher (`Level.toughness`, `MongolSoldier.toughen`; not a boss); each level left behind adds 10 health (`AbbasidGame.VIGOUR`). Soldiers answer the new moves (`EnemyBrain`: `answer_charge` (a veteran steps back, a spearman thrusts), `_wary` after two whirls or near an Art; `EnemyProfile.art_resistance` for the Captain). A playtest log (`PlayLog`, `shared/playtest/`, fed by the session in words: `play_log.event`/`count`/`add`/`health`) writes every street entered, blow swung and taken (its warning and the answer; every soldier's blow begun at the hero is counted, so one never touching him is told as avoided; a blow with no attack of its own is named by `HitData.cause`: arrow, fire pot, fire), his lowest health on each street, the remedies drunk, the damage each of his moves dealt, kill, fall, Art, finisher, lamp, lesson and card to `user://playlogs/<start>.csv`, and a summary per street to `<start>.txt` when a street is left, the game paused or closed (`GameSettings.playtest_log`, on; off while the checks run unless `PlayLog.in_checks`). Progress is a `SaveGame` (`user://save.cfg`); settings a `GameSettings` (`user://settings.cfg`: volumes, display, access options (`flashes` → `Combatant.flash_scale` and `VfxDirector.calm`, `time_effects` → `HitStop.enabled`, `colourblind` → `TELL_COLOURS_CLEAR`), lessons mode, and the player's `bindings`, applied to the InputMap by `GameSettings.rebind`/`_apply_bindings`); the `ABBASID_USER_PREFIX` environment variable (set by `tools/run_godot_cli.mjs`) keeps checks away from the player's files.

## Art pipeline
- Characters are 3D models rendered into pixel art, all in Node (no Blender): `lib/meshes.mjs` (lofted tubes, ellipsoids, lathes, shields, ribbons), `characters/body3d.mjs` (the skeleton: hips, twisting torso, head, two-bone IK legs and arms, a centre-gripped shield, blade direction; skinning; cloth chains simulated through each animation), `characters/figure3d.mjs` (shared parts: torso, bands, a skirt that follows the thighs, sleeves, trousers, boots), `lib/raster.mjs` (a G-buffer at 4 samples a pixel, camera turned 22° to a three-quarter view) and `lib/sprite_shader.mjs` (majority downsampling, palette ramps by light, separation lines, warm back rim, outline). Models: `yusuf.mjs`, `mongol3d.mjs` (swordsman, spearman, archer, captain at 1.14×, veteran, maceman at 1.06×, shieldbearer with a `towerBoard` shield, engineer with clay pots; a blade can be `hidden`: its line is only for the hitboxes, as the mace head's), `townsfolk3d.mjs`; animations: `yusuf_animations.mjs`, `mongol3d_animations.mjs`, `townsfolk3d_animations.mjs`, helpers in `pose3d.mjs`. The hero's blows are posed with `armOut` (a hand a set length from its shoulder along a direction), `toward` (a blade's [angle, yaw] from a direction), `wheel`/`spinPose` (a turn with the blade on a tilted wheel and the feet turned with the hips) and `cleaveLanding` (a deep lunge, head up, shield flung back); every new move gets its own silhouette, never a folded body or an arm hidden behind the shield, and a strike frame keeps the blade's sweep close before his chest. A finisher's two halves share one timing table (`characters/finisher_timing.mjs`: frame durations, distance, cut/burst/death frames), which also writes its `FinisherDefinition`. `render3d.mjs` draws blades as two-pixel lines (hidden behind nearer parts) and exports blade hilt/tip per frame for the hitboxes; sword trails are not in the sprites but drawn at runtime by `features/combat/sword_trail.gd` from those blade positions (a band over the blade's outer half in the blow's warning colour, fading in 0.13 s, so the blade, the arm and the man struck stay visible). A blow's hitbox is the sweep between its frames' blades, cut off behind his back unless the animation is `allRound` (the sweep, the whirl, the Storm). `lib/root_motion.mjs` holds planted feet still while an attack's lunge (read from its `AttackDefinition`) carries the body. Every canvas is 128 px tall with the feet 4 px above its bottom (sprite offset (0, -60)); widths 96–208 by action. A pose can `hide` parts (a sabre put away to loot) and `show` optional ones (the codex a soldier throws on the pyre, a wound's raw cap). An animation's `wound` (frames => point) is exported per frame; `renderPiece` draws a cut-off piece tumbling through turns. Each soldier (and each townsperson who can be executed) gets a GoreSet (`<prefix>_gore.tres`: pieces' SpriteFrames, their resting heights and origins, the pieces each cut throws, the wounds); the dead of the streets are props (`CORPSES` in `townsfolk3d_animations.mjs`, drawn by `environment/props3d.mjs`).
- Character art follows `docs/art_style_guide.md` (scale, light, value groups, faction colours, silhouettes, animation and effect rules). Check animations with `node tools/animation_lint.mjs` (pops, foot sliding, loop seams, the ground speeds `walk_animation_speed`/`run_animation_speed` must use; it leaves out parts a cut has hidden and lets an attack's live frames, a throw's release and a finisher's blows (its cuts, bursts and `strikes` in `finisher_timing.mjs`) snap; the known issues are kept by cause in `tools/animation_lint_baseline.json` (one, accepted), `--check` fails on a new or worse one, `--update` after mending), the cast in each level with `tests/capture_cast.gd`, and their timing in `captures/animation_reel.html` (`node tools/review_reel.mjs`: everyone built). Soldiers share animation names, so each soldier's lunges (for planted feet) and live frames come from his own profile (`readAttacks(<profile>.tres)` in `lib/root_motion.mjs`). `build_characters.mjs --only <who> --anim a,b` rebuilds some animations and keeps the rest of the data (`--out <dir>` builds elsewhere).
- Distant layers come from the user's concept paintings: `environment/painted.mjs` resamples a crop of a painting (named by the level's `painting` entry) to the game's grid and reduces it to a palette (`lib/quantize.mjs`); the level's street backdrop is then mapped to that palette, built lower and darker so the painted city shows above it. The camera sits 84 px above the hero's feet so the city fills the upper half of the frame. `features/presentation/post_process.gdshader` (layer 5, over the world, under the HUD) adds warm bloom, split toning and a vignette; the fighters' shader (`features/combat/hit_flash.gdshader`) lifts them above the scene and adds a firelit rim. No ordered dithering anywhere: gradients are smooth. Review with `tests/capture_tour.gd` (every level through the real game view).
- Environment painters (for what the paintings do not cover): `environment/layers.mjs` (sky, skyline, city, river; one `LOOKS` entry per hour: night, smoke, predawn, dawn), `backdrop.mjs` (the street-side facades by section theme: houses, ruin, hammam, mosque, khan, potters/spices/books, darb, portal, madrasa, library, river, rampart, gatehouse, camp; painted with relief, `relief.mjs`: the painters write how far each pixel stands out or sinks (door and window recesses, cornices, sills, balconies on their brackets, awnings, mortar, fallen plaster showing brick) and the street is lit from it: a sky from above that projections shut out, the fires and lamps lighting the faces turned to them with their shadows, recesses dark near their rims, what burns keeping its colour.
