# The recorded kit: credits

Every physical sound in the game (blades, shields, bodies, steps, armour, cloth, arrows, voices) is layered from
these recordings by `tools/asset_generation/audio/build_sounds.mjs`. All are dedicated to the public domain (CC0
1.0): no attribution is required, but it is given gladly. Music, ambience, the interface and the Arts' sounds are
made in code.

- **Medieval Sound Effects: Weapon on Weapon** by Still North Media (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/still_north_impacts/`: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/medieval_sfx_weapon_on_weapon_1_of_2.7z (and _2_of_2)
- **Medieval Sound Effects: Weapon Textures** by Still North Media (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/still_north_textures/`: https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-textures
- **Impact Sounds** by Kenney (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/kenney_impact/`: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- **RPG Audio** by Kenney (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/kenney_rpg/`: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- **RPG Sound Pack** by artisticdude (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/artisticdude_rpg/`: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
- **37 hits/punches** by Independent.nu (submitted by qubodup) (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/inu_hits/`: https://opengameart.org/content/37-hitspunches
- **75 CC0 breaking/falling/hit sfx** by rubberduck (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/rubberduck_breaking/`: https://opengameart.org/content/75-cc0-breaking-falling-hit-sfx
- **Male Grunt/Yelling Sounds** by HaelDB (CC0 1.0), `sources/audio/haeldb_voices/`: https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds

The raw libraries are not kept in the repository (`sources/` is git-ignored); `build_kit.mjs` cuts this kit from
them. Each take below names its recording and the seconds it spans there.
