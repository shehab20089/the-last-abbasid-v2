# The Last Abbasid: combat focus — one job for every move

**Status (2026-10-10): built (all five steps); awaiting the user's playtest, read through the playtest log. See Progress at the end.**

The user asked what is wrong with the combat. Nothing is broken; the trouble is width without depth. Two audits
(every move of Yusuf's, every soldier and every fight in the four levels) found:

- **About 35 actions in one chapter**, 13 of them from the first step of the Fallen Market.
- **Many moves doing the same job.**
  - About a dozen get past a raised round shield: the cleave, the pommel strike, the executioner's cleave,
    the rising cleave, the windmill, the running thrust, the bash, the charged cleaves and the plunge, with the
    low cut and the sweep going under it and the delayed cut fooling it.
  - Six answer a crowd: the whirl, the sweep, the windmill, the Storm, the third charge, the Second Wind's cry.
  - About ten close distance.

  When four moves solve the same problem, choosing between them is no decision, and fights play the same
  whichever is used ("the game feels repetitive").
- **Moves taught where nothing needs them.** The charged cleave comes from Hamid at the very start of the Market,
  which holds no shield wall and no big man. The kick is taught before a terrace with nothing to kick off. The
  guarded thrust and the down-stab are taught where no fight asks for them. The library's men, the plunge's own
  lesson, cannot be surprised from the gallery as its page promises.

## The rules

1. **One job per move.** Each move is the best answer to one situation no other move answers. Two answers to one
   situation are kept only when they differ in risk (the slow cleave that breaks a guard, the quick low cut that
   passes under it).
2. **The enemies demand the jobs.** A move stays only if some fight in Chapter I asks for it.
3. **A move arrives just before the enemy that needs it.** The Fallen Market starts with the core; each street adds
   a few.
4. **Nothing is thrown away.** The moves whose jobs others do become the **master's techniques**: finishing
   Chapter I opens them for every later journey, as the deeper layer of a replay (and of the chapters and
   weapons to come).

## The buttons (the template for every weapon)

| Button | Its role | With a context |
|---|---|---|
| Light | the cutting string | down: under a guard; on the run: closing in; in the air: the air slash; late in a roll: the counter |
| Heavy | the committed blow, which breaks a guard | held: the charge; down: the sweep; in the air: the plunge; shield up: the bash; over a man down: the ground stroke; on a staggered man: the finisher |
| Block | the guard; timed, the parry, then light for the riposte | in a blow's glint: Steady Breath |
| Dodge | the roll | under a blow: the Close Call |
| Throw, Art | knives; the Arts paid with resolve (two carried) | |

A new weapon (the spear, the long axe, the bow) fills the same roles its own way.

The tree shows them locked with what opens them, so a first journey sees what waits. A finished chapter is
remembered apart from the save (`user://records.cfg`), so a new game after it is a master's journey.

## Making the enemies demand the jobs

- **The library** (Scholars' Quarter): its four men stay at their tasks and can be surprised. Today a dormant group
  is never unaware, so the plunge from the gallery is an ordinary blow, against its own page. Visible men busy at a
  task become unaware like any other; hidden ambushers stay as they are.
- **The kick** is taught where the first engineer's fire falls among soldiers. Its card says it: drive a man into
  the fire or off a ledge.
- **The charge** is taught just before the axeman, whose poise a held cleave breaks, with the shield-bearer
  beyond it.
- **Hamid's counsel** is now the parry and riposte, taught before the first duel; his words change to match.

## How we will know

The playtest log (`shared/playtest/play_log.gd`, written to `playlogs` in the game's data folder) shows, per
street:
- the blows Yusuf swung;
- each warning and how it was answered;
- what felled him.

Each kept move should appear where its enemy is. A move nobody uses has no job yet.

## Building it

1. The schedule in the level data: the lessons, pages and Hamid's counsel; what each street knows on entry.
2. The master's techniques: the finished-chapter record, the tree's locks (Judgment and the Unseen freed of the
   moves above them), the lessons only shown on a master's journey (`StoryTrigger.requires_flag`).
3. The enemies: the library's men; the kick and the charge where their soldiers are.
4. The teaching: Hamid's card and words (English and Arabic), each move's "where it comes from" on the Techniques
   page, the lock text on the tree.
5. Tests, the documents, the build.

## Progress

### Built (2026-10-10)

- **The schedule.**
  - The level data (`tools/levels/*.mjs`, rebuilt) teaches each move as in the list above. Hamid's Market counsel is `counsel: "riposte"`. The Market's `lesson_charge` and `lesson_kick` are gone.
  - The Scholars' Quarter has `lesson_kick` (c158–161) and `lesson_charge` (c214–217).
  - The master's lessons carry `requiresFlag: "master"`.
  - Each street's `knownTechniques` lists only what the streets before it taught.
- **The master's techniques.**
  - **Record:** `SaveGame.record_master` / `is_master` (`user://records.cfg`); `AbbasidGame._show_complete` records it, and `_master_journey` sets the `master` flag on a new game or a continued one.
  - **Tree:** the five tree nodes wait on `master` (`LOCK_MASTER`). The Judgment no longer needs the executioner above it, and the Unseen needs Death from Above.
  - **Lessons:** `StoryTrigger.requires_flag` (built from `requiresFlag`) silences a trigger without its flag.
  - **Techniques page:** each move says where it comes from (`SOURCE_MASTER_TREE`, `SOURCE_MASTER_LESSON`, `SOURCE_KICK`, `SOURCE_CHARGE`).
- **The enemies:** the library's four stay at their work (patrol 0).
  - A man of a dormant group busy at a task in plain sight is taken unawares, and sees, hears and answers alarms as any busy soldier does (`EnemyBrain._at_work_in_sight`). Hidden ambushers wait blind and ready until sprung.
  - Before this, the level autoplayer showed the gap: a looter it passed above, on the gallery, never woke, since only the street-level trigger springs the group, and the level could not be finished.
- **The teaching:** a counsel card (`Npc.counsel`, `AbbasidGame._counsel`, the card kind `counsel`: `LESSON_COUNSEL_HEADING`, `COUNSEL_RIPOSTE_TITLE`, `HINT_COUNSEL_RIPOSTE`, the riposte demo). Hamid's words (`GUARD_6`) and the charge's lesson (`HINT_LEARNED_CHARGE`, now naming a big man's poise) are rewritten in both languages.
- **The playtest log:** `shared/playtest/play_log.gd`, fed by the session (`play_log.event`/`count`), with the Settings switch `Playtest Log`.
- **Tests:** session 239.

### Where the build departs from the plan

- **The kick's lesson is at c158–161, not c176.** At c176 it fell inside the river wall's 3-second execution clock (from c164); before the lamp at c160 it comes in a quiet stretch, about 35 columns before the first engineer's fire.
- **The charge's lesson is at c214–217, past the axeman, not before him.** A lesson card waits for a quiet moment, and the axeman is alert from about c198, so a lesson before him would show only after his fight. Its first job is the second shield wall (c258, from a step away), then the mace-bearer and the axeman at the Last Gate and the Captain. The axeman's own answer is the parry and the roll.
